# Project Specification

This system is designed to emulate the functionality of a Carbon Credit Tracking System that would allow employers to track their employees' carbon usage and earn Carbon Credits. Employees would be able to utilize their mobile or desktop web-browser to enter their individual trips from their starting location to their final destination and the method of travel they used to get there; taking public transport, ride-sharing/taxiing/carpooling, or working from home would all provide the employee more points towards their employer's Carbon Credit balance.

Employer administrators would be able to not only track their own trips (as they are also employees), but also they could review the point generation of each of their fellow employees. Employers can then convert the total points earned into Carbon Credits.

Global administrators would be able to create trips for individual employees, review the trips for a given employer, or even create users via request.

# Technology Stack
